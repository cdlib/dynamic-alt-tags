# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project aims to be a respectful, constructive place to collaborate.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent, but be clear and direct.
- Focus feedback on the code, behavior, or proposal rather than the person.
- Be welcoming to contributors with different levels of experience.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or hateful language
- Trolling, intimidation, or sustained hostility
- Publishing private information without permission

## Maintainer responsibility

The maintainer may moderate discussions, close conversations that are unproductive, and reject contributions that are not a good fit for the project.

## Reporting conduct issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email `esatzman@ucop.edu`.
